# Files, search, and memory

## Where your companion keeps its work

Your desktop has a folder named **joshu's files**. Your companion keeps all of its work there, in plain files that you can open, read, and change. The most important folders are:

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `Projects` | One folder for each of your projects, for example `Projects/harbor-client`. Each project has `about.md` (the goal, deadline, and status), `todo.md` (the next steps and who you are waiting on), and a journal with one file for each day. |
| `Projects/other` | Things that do not belong to a project yet. |
| `Projects/_archive` | Finished projects. |
| `Planning` | Your daily capture list (ideas and tasks that you mention in chat or by phone), your morning and evening review, and your time-block plan for the day. |
| `Triage` | Emails that your companion still has to file. Each note points to an email. |
| `connectors` | Copies of your mail and calendar that your companion keeps up to date. |
| `Teams` | Your notes for [Multiplayer](08-boss-mode.md) teams. |

### How email becomes project work

When an important email arrives, your companion files it into the right project. It adds a line to the project's `todo.md` or journal, with a link back to the email. If somebody owes you an answer, your companion keeps track of it until the answer comes.

### Project status

Each project has a status in its `about.md`: **active**, **someday**, **reference**, or **done**. Your companion reminds you only about active projects. To change a status, tell your companion, for example "Put the website project on someday."

### PDFs and text files

You can drop a PDF or a `.txt` file anywhere on your desktop. Your companion makes a readable copy next to it (for example `report.md` next to `report.pdf`) so that it can search and quote the file. Scanned PDFs that contain only pictures of text cannot be read.

## Search everything with File Brain

The **File Brain** app searches everything on your desktop, your mail copies included. You can type keywords, or ask a question in plain words, for example "What did the Harbor contract say about payment terms?". New and changed files can be found within seconds.

While File Brain updates its index, a small message appears at the top right of your desktop and then says "Indexing finished". Right after your box restarts, File Brain can show no pages for a few minutes while it rebuilds.

Your companion uses the same search when you ask it a question, so you rarely need to open File Brain yourself.

## What your companion remembers

Your companion remembers your conversations: your preferences, the people you mention, and what you talked about last week. It saves and recalls these memories by itself, on every channel.

The **Memory** app shows what your companion remembers, as a map of linked memories or as a table. The Memory app is for looking only. If your companion remembers something wrong, tell it the right fact in chat, for example "Sam is my business partner, not my brother."
