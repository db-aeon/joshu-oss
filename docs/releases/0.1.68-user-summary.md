# Joshu OSS 0.1.68: what's in this release

## Run Joshu as one container

You can now run Joshu with one `docker run` command and one disk. You give your box its web address with a Cloudflare Tunnel, which needs no open ports, or with your own web server. Docker Compose still works in this release, but it is deprecated, and a later release will remove it. The [self-host guide](../self-host.md) has the steps for a new box and for moving a Compose box.

## Your companion checks with you before it answers strangers

When someone you don't know emails your companion, e.g., to set up a meeting, your companion now asks you first. It does not offer your free times until you say yes.

## Research reports have news, papers, and company filings again

Research reports again include news stories, research papers, company filings, and stock prices, because the service that Joshu used for them closed and the box now finds a new one by itself. These sources need the content router key, and each search takes a small amount from its balance.

## Better web search

If you add a You.com key, your companion searches the web with You.com, and it uses the usual search when You.com has no answer.

## Your companion remembers your rules better

Your companion has more room for what it remembers about you, and it keeps your personal rules apart from its own notes.

## Other changes

- Long requests no longer go quiet when the companion takes many steps.
- Messages from your companion keep their words and paragraphs whole.
- Your desktop has the new File Manager and System Settings, and works better on a phone.
- jNotes uses the full width of its window, and you can pinch to zoom on a page that your companion hands you on your phone.
- Setup tips close by themselves when you have done the step.
- Your companion runs on newer models, and Joshu deletes old background work records after 30 days.
