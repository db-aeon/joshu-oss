# Release notes: 0.1.59 → 0.1.65 (OSS)

Git tag: `v0.1.65-oss`

| Image | Ref |
|-------|-----|
| Sandbox | `ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss:0.1.65` |
| Voice (optional `voice-rt` profile) | `ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss-voice-realtime:0.1.65` |
| Instance agent (optional `fleet` profile) | `ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss-instance-agent:0.1.65` |

Pin: [`deploy/RELEASE.json`](../../deploy/RELEASE.json)

Example env: [`deploy/.env.vps.example`](../../deploy/.env.vps.example)

Hermes (`3c27eb6`) and gbrain (`fa2c7a6`) are unchanged. Postgres for Hindsight is still version 17.

Plain-language summary: section [User summary](#user-summary) at the end of this file.

0.1.65 is the first open-source release since 0.1.59, so these notes cover the work of six releases (0.1.60 to 0.1.65). The compose file does not change, so the upgrade is a normal image upgrade. Three changes affect how you run a box:

- Research reports, recurring reports, and the new Research app replace the last30days app. The web search of a report needs an Exa key (`EXA_API_KEY`).
- The Joshu content router is a new, optional metered provider. With a router API key (`JOSHU_ROUTER_API_KEY`), the companion can read social posts, make images and videos, add social sources to reports, and, new in 0.1.65, use You.com in place of Exa for the web search of a Research report.
- The routes under `/joshu/api/connectors`, `/joshu/api/nylas`, and `/joshu/api/onboarding` now need the ArozOS desktop session of the owner or a direct localhost request. Before, anyone who could reach the box could call them.

Read [Upgrade notes](#upgrade-notes-from-0159) before you upgrade a box.

## What changed since 0.1.59

### Research reports and the Research app

The owner can ask the companion for research on any topic, on any channel (text message, email, jChat, Slack, Telegram, or a phone call). The owner gets a short summary and a PDF report in the same conversation, usually within 10 minutes. See [research-reports.md](../technical-reference/research-reports.md) for the full pipeline and [10-research-reports.md](../user-manual/10-research-reports.md) for the owner's view.

- How a report is made. One model call plans the research, with sub-questions, what the topic means and does not mean, and search terms. Exa finds the pages, and one model call for each theme extracts facts. A fact is kept only when its quote is in the text of its page. Code drops each sentence that cites an unknown fact or has a number that is not in a cited quote. Headless Chromium in the image prints the PDF.
- Themes. A report starts with background themes and then has news themes from the dated pages and social posts of the last 30 days. Each theme has a headline, one to three paragraphs, and its links. A short summary sits at the top.
- Search terms. The plan adds named terms (the companies, products, and people that the owner named) and expanded terms (for example competitors). Each term gets its own search, so the report covers each name that the owner asked about.
- Sections. The owner can name up to 6 sections. The companion passes sections only when the owner asks for a fixed structure, and other guidance goes into the notes. Each section keeps the owner's words as its label and gets a written headline.
- Storage and delivery. The box saves `report.pdf`, `report.html`, and `report.json` in `<files>/Research/<date>-<topic>/`. Email gets a reply with the PDF attached. Text messages and phone calls get a signed report link (`/joshu/api/research/r/...`) that opens the PDF without a sign-in.
- Models. All research model calls go through OpenRouter with no-training routing. The model is `JOSHU_RESEARCH_WRITER_MODEL`, and the default is `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`.
- Extra sources. When the box has a Composio key, Composio Search adds Google News, Google Scholar, and, for a public company, a price page and the newest SEC filings. The toolkit is pinned to `20260903_00` (override `JOSHU_COMPOSIO_SEARCH_TOOLKIT_VERSION`). A failed Composio call only gives fewer pages.
- Social sources. Posts from Reddit, X, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and other sources come through the content router (see below). Without a router API key, a report has web and news only, and its source list says so.
- Group step. A report on a busy topic keeps its news themes. The group step gets a larger output limit and 60 seconds, and when it still fails, the box logs `[research] group step failed` and makes each dated news page its own story.

Recurring reports send regular updates on one or more topics. The owner sets one up in one message or in the Research app. A Hermes cron job with no LLM starts each run. A run collects what is new since the last run, sorts it into themes, and sends the update by email (the whole update in the body, with the PDF attached) or, when the owner asks, by text message, Slack, or Telegram. A run with nothing new sends nothing. A repeat request on the topic of an earlier Research report gives an update against that report. Recurring reports always use Exa for web search.

The Research desktop app lists every report and recurring report and can create, change, pause, and delete them. See [research-arozos-app.md](../research-arozos-app.md).

### You.com web search for a Research report (new in 0.1.65)

A one-time Research report can use You.com in place of Exa for its web search.

- Tool `research_report_make` of plugin `joshu-research` has a new optional argument `web_provider`, with the value `exa` (the default) or `you.com`. `POST /joshu/api/research/reports` takes the same field and answers HTTP 400 for any other value. Skill `research-report` tells the companion when to send it.
- The box calls the content router endpoint `you.web.search.highlights` (`src/research/pipeline/you.ts`), with a time limit of 30 seconds for each search and one retry after a rate limit. It still runs at most four searches at once.
- You.com needs the content router. On a self-hosted box, that means a router API key (see [Content router and metered providers](#content-router-and-metered-providers)). When the box cannot call the router, the report fails at step "search" with the reason, and the box does not fall back to Exa.
- Exa still reads the full text and the images of the best pages, the filings, and the pages of each theme. A box with You.com and no Exa key gets only the short passages that You.com returns, so its reports have less detail.
- The source list of the report says "Web (You.com)" or "Web (Exa)".
- Each You.com search costs the provider price ($0.0055 at release time) plus the router markup, from the usage wallet of your router account. A report makes about 12 to 28 web searches.
- Recurring reports, report updates, and the Hermes web search backend (`web.backend: exa`) keep Exa.

### Content router and metered providers

The Joshu content router is a paid, hosted API at `https://hello.joshu.me`. It is optional. Each call takes the provider price plus a router markup from the usage wallet of your account there. Without it, Research reports still work with Exa, and the features below are off. See [metered-providers.md](../technical-reference/metered-providers.md).

- Provider `router` joins fal.ai as a metered provider. On a self-hosted box it uses direct mode with `JOSHU_ROUTER_API_KEY`, or a key that the owner pastes in Connectors, section AI providers. You create the key in your account on hello.joshu.me (`https://hello.joshu.me/router/keys`). Hermes gets the MCP server `joshu_router` and the toolset `mcp-joshu-router` only when a key is saved. The local relay listens on port 8798.
- Content goals. The social sources of reports are goals, for example "YouTube videos: humanoid robots". Each box learns which router endpoint serves a goal (route cache) and how to read its reply (shape cache), and keeps both as JSON in its own skill `joshu-content-cache` under `$HERMES_HOME/skills/`. A shape recipe is made of JSON paths, never code. A goal kind that fails is not tried again for 7 days.
- Empty optional fields (fixed in 0.1.65). A shape recipe with an empty list for an optional field, for example `"author": []` for web search results, was refused as a whole, so the goal showed "could not be read". The box now reads an empty list as "no field". The link and the title stay required. The Joshu API now logs the reason, as `[content-router] goal <kind>: <endpoint> could not be read: <reason>`.
- Hints. `research_report_make` and `research_recurring_save` take a `hints` argument, for example a preferred source for a kind of question. New factory skill `joshu-content-router` has the default hints.
- Social post links. New factory skill `joshu-social-posts` reads the details, transcript, or comments of an Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, or LinkedIn link through the router, and falls back to the shared browser when the router is off.
- Images and videos. The new Hermes plugin `joshu-media` sends image and video requests to `POST /joshu/api/router/media`, and the box saves the file on the Desktop. Hermes enables the plugin and its image and video tools only while the content router is on.
- Limits. Goals use only endpoints with a fixed price. Model inputs are checked against the endpoint schema. A report has at most 5 goal sources. Media downloads use https from public hosts only, with a size limit.
- Mode. When `JOSHU_<ID>_MODE` is not set, a provider uses direct mode, unless the box has both `CONTROL_PLANE_URL` and `INSTANCE_AGENT_TOKEN`, in which case it uses the relay of that control plane. `JOSHU_<ID>_MODE=off` turns a provider off.

### Security

- Connectors, Nylas, and onboarding routes. Caddy sends each `/joshu/*` request to the Joshu API, and these routes did not check the caller, so a request from the internet could search the mail mirror, disconnect a connection, or change the owner channel. A new middleware (`src/connectors/apiAccess.ts`) answers `401 {"error":"desktop_session_required"}` to any request without the desktop session that is not a direct localhost request. Callers on the box use localhost and keep working, and jMail, Connectors, and Welcome send the desktop session.
- `POST /api/nylas/agent/import` stays open for a control plane push, but it saves a grant only when the box has none or when the grant id and email match. Any other push gets `409 grant_already_set`.
- `follow-redirects` 1.16.2 (GHSA-mrwp-f3q8-3g9p, credentials sent to another host after a redirect) in the root and runtime lockfiles.
- `pypdf` 6.21.4 in the Browser Use environment (`/opt/browser/venv`), for seven advisories where a crafted PDF can make the parser run for a long time or use much memory.
- The image no longer ships the npm dev tools of the Hermes dashboard build (about 300 MB under `/opt/hermes-agent/node_modules`, which included `undici` 7.28.0 with GHSA-xjj6-f33q-929c).
- Every OpenRouter request that Joshu makes for an owner excludes the upstream provider Relace, which dropped the arguments of tool calls of `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`. Joshu adds `relace` to `provider_routing.ignore` in the Hermes config, to the other OpenRouter requests of Hermes, and to the direct calls of the Joshu API, and keeps any slugs that are already there.

### Owner messages and realtime goals

- A slow SMS reply no longer triggers a false "I restarted before I could finish, please resend" text. Each run of the realtime goal broker has its own id, and only owner texts of an earlier run get that text.
- The SMS router now sees each text that Joshu delivered (nudges, reminders, team texts), and a cancel needs a named active goal. Before, a reply such as "you can cancel" could cancel the wrong goal.
- The owner outbox reads the Slack or Telegram bot token from the Joshu environment and the safety settings, and else from `~/.hermes/.env`. After two failed sends on Slack or Telegram, it texts the owner mobile instead.
- `vps-start.sh` and the Hermes dotenv sync keep one line for each key that they write. Before, each start added new copies of some lines in `~/.hermes/.env`.
- In a Slack group DM, the owner can address the companion by name without an @mention. A top-level mention also gets the last 10 messages of the last 6 hours as context. Messages of other members keep the mention rule. The new patch `scripts/patch-hermes-slack-group-dm.py` runs at image build and at each start.
- A realtime goal from Slack or Telegram keeps the files of the message (at most 10, each up to 25 MB) and the chat context. The broker copies the files under `<state dir>/files/`.
- When mail ingress handles a request from an owner address that matches an open realtime goal, the new MCP tool `realtime_goal_close_done_elsewhere` closes the goal and tells the owner on the channel of the goal.
- A blocked realtime goal with no owner answer for 48 hours is cancelled without a message (`JOSHU_REALTIME_GOALS_BLOCKED_EXPIRY_HOURS`).
- App chat works for files in folders. Hermes rejected a session id with a `/`, so the chat of a note in a folder gave no answer. The chat panel now also shows a failed run as a short error.
- Numbered lists in agent mail keep their numbers when blank lines split the items.

### Mail and calendar

- Live Gmail search. The new endpoint `GET /joshu/api/connectors/mail/gmail/live-search?q=` and the MCP tools `gmail_search_live` and `gmail_get_message` search each enabled owner Gmail account directly through Composio, also Updates, Promotions, spam, and trash, which the Gmail sync skips. Skill `joshu-mail` 1.8.0 adds it to the mail search order.
- Gmail drafts. The MCP tool policy now allows `GMAIL_CREATE_EMAIL_DRAFT`, `GMAIL_LIST_DRAFTS`, and `GMAIL_GET_DRAFT`. Send, reply, forward, `GMAIL_SEND_DRAFT`, `GMAIL_UPDATE_DRAFT`, and `GMAIL_DELETE_DRAFT` stay blocked. The action guard, when it is on, still asks the owner first.
- Smaller mail worker prompts. The mail sections of `ea-playbook` move into the new factory skills `ea-mail-track` and `ea-mail-ingress`. A track run sends about 78% fewer preloaded skill tokens on each LLM call, and an ingress run about 31% fewer. Hermes cron jobs still preload `ea-playbook`.
- When an event that Joshu creates or updates has guests, the Joshu API adds the owner to the guest list, because Google does not list the organizer of an event that the API makes.
- The Google Calendar events and free-slots routes answer 400 for a bad `date` instead of crashing the stack. They accept `today`, `tomorrow`, and `yesterday` in the time zone of the request. The Joshu API now logs an unhandled promise rejection instead of exiting.

### Companion skills, Kanban, and browser

- New factory skill `joshu-manual`. The image does not carry the user manual, so the companion reads the chapter from `https://www.joshu.me/md/manual/<slug>` with `curl`, and uses the self-host text when `JOSHU_STANDALONE` is `1` or `true`. The box needs outbound access to www.joshu.me for this.
- Each start copies every Hermes plugin of the image into `$HERMES_HOME/plugins`, so Kanban workers, which start outside `/opt/joshu`, get the research tools and the browser handoff tools.
- A Kanban worker that closes its own task with `hermes kanban complete` or `hermes kanban block` no longer kills itself. The patch now skips the stop signal when the caller is the worker of that task.
- A task blocked with block kind `dependency` and no open parent task stays blocked, instead of going back to `ready` and starting an empty worker run.
- The release facts check job can now change Hermes memory with the memory tool, and it also checks the skills that the box made. The job now gets only the toolsets `memory`, `file`, `skills`, and `no_mcp`.
- Shared browser. On a bot check, Hermes no longer suggests a Browserbase upgrade. On Browser Use Cloud, the advice is to wait about 10 seconds for the captcha solver and then request a browser handoff. On local Chromium, the advice is to request a browser handoff. Skill `joshu-browser-handoff` 1.11.0 has a new section on bot checks.
- PDFs open in the PDF Viewer instead of the Markdown editor.

### Instance agent

The box diagnostics query `hermes_config` now reads the config with the Python of the Hermes venv (`/opt/hermes-agent/venv/bin/python`, or `JOSHU_HERMES_PYTHON`), because the system Python has no PyYAML. Before, it always answered `ok: false`. You need this only if you run the `fleet` profile with your own control plane.

### Removed

- The last30days app, its Watch feature, plugin `joshu-last30days`, its skills, its REST API, its two watch cron jobs, and its engine.
- Python 3.12 under `/opt/joshu/.local/python312`, which only the last30days engine used.
- The env keys `LAST30DAYS_PYTHON`, `LAST30DAYS_IMAGE_ENGINE_ROOT`, and `SCRAPECREATORS_API_KEY`. No code reads them.
- The fixed ScrapeCreators and Xquik readers of the social sources. The content router replaces them.
- The rule that cancelled the only active realtime goal when the router named none.

## Upgrade notes (from 0.1.59)

The compose file and the volumes do not change, and Postgres stays on version 17, so Hindsight needs no migration.

First, decide which keys you want to add to `/etc/joshu/instance.env`:

- `EXA_API_KEY` is needed for Research reports with Exa and for recurring reports. Without it, both fail at step "search". It was optional before.
- `JOSHU_ROUTER_API_KEY` is optional. It turns on the social sources, social post links, images, videos, and You.com web search. The owner can also paste it later in Connectors, section AI providers.
- `COMPOSIO_API_KEY` is optional, as before. It adds Google News, Google Scholar, and company data to reports, and it is needed for the live Gmail search.

If you run the `fleet` profile with your own control plane, the box has `CONTROL_PLANE_URL` and `INSTANCE_AGENT_TOKEN`, so fal.ai and the content router now default to relay mode through that control plane. Set `JOSHU_FAL_MODE=direct` and `JOSHU_ROUTER_MODE=direct` if your control plane has no metered relay.

You can delete `LAST30DAYS_PYTHON`, `LAST30DAYS_IMAGE_ENGINE_ROOT`, and `SCRAPECREATORS_API_KEY`, because no code reads them.

Second, set the release pins:

```bash
sudo sed -i 's|^JOSHU_RELEASE_VERSION=.*|JOSHU_RELEASE_VERSION=0.1.65|' /etc/joshu/instance.env
sudo sed -i 's|^JOSHU_IMAGE_REF=.*|JOSHU_IMAGE_REF=ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss:0.1.65|' /etc/joshu/instance.env
sudo sed -i 's|^JOSHU_VOICE_IMAGE_REF=.*|JOSHU_VOICE_IMAGE_REF=ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss-voice-realtime:0.1.65|' /etc/joshu/instance.env
```

If you run the instance agent, also set `JOSHU_INSTANCE_AGENT_IMAGE_REF=ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss-instance-agent:0.1.65`.

Third, pull the images and write the host bundle of 0.1.65 to `/opt/joshu/deploy/`:

```bash
docker pull ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss:0.1.65
docker pull ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss-voice-realtime:0.1.65

docker run --rm --entrypoint cat ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss:0.1.65 \
  /opt/joshu/.image/host/deploy/scripts/install-host-bundle.sh \
  | sudo JOSHU_IMAGE_REF=ghcr.io/db-aeon/joshu-oss:0.1.65 bash -s
```

Fourth, recreate the services:

```bash
cd /opt/joshu/deploy
docker compose -f docker-compose.yml --env-file /etc/joshu/instance.env up -d --force-recreate caddy joshu-stack
docker compose -f docker-compose.yml --env-file /etc/joshu/instance.env --profile voice-rt up -d --force-recreate voice-realtime
```

Fifth, when the stack is healthy, you can remove old release images with `bash /opt/joshu/deploy/scripts/prune-release-images.sh` (`DRY_RUN=1` lists what it would remove).

### What happens at the first start

- last30days. The Joshu API removes the copies of the last30days app, its plugin, its skills, and its two watch cron jobs. It keeps the old results in `research/last30days/` and the engine state in `.joshu/last30days`. If the box had Watch topics, they become one recurring report named "Watched topics", with the schedule and channel of the Watch cron job, and one model call turns the prompt of that job into notes. A marker file makes the migration run once. The recurring report needs `EXA_API_KEY` to run.
- Hermes plugins. Each start copies every plugin of the image into `$HERMES_HOME/plugins`. Hermes enables `joshu-research`, and it enables `joshu-media` only when the content router is on.
- Skills seed. The factory skills changed, so the skills seed runs and does an LLM merge for each box skill with box changes. New factory skills are `joshu-manual`, `joshu-social-posts`, `joshu-content-router`, `ea-mail-track`, `ea-mail-ingress`, and `research-report`. When the seed first copies the split of `ea-playbook`, it moves the box pitfalls of the moved sections into the new mail skills.
- Release facts. The stamp format changed, so the release facts check job checks every release fact once more.
- Hermes config and patches. `provider_routing.ignore` gets `relace`. The Kanban stop patch, the dependency block patch, the Slack group DM patch, and the browser guard patch (marker `joshu_cloud_browser_ensure_v4`) are applied. `~/.hermes/.env` loses its duplicate lines.
- Realtime goals. The first lifecycle tick cancels each blocked realtime goal with no owner answer for 48 hours, without a message.
- Kanban. A mail track made before the upgrade gets its preloaded skills set to `ea-mail-track` at its next handoff.
- Desktop. The Research app appears, and the last30days app goes away.

### API changes that can affect your scripts

- Every route under `/joshu/api/connectors`, `/joshu/api/nylas`, and `/joshu/api/onboarding` returns `401 {"error":"desktop_session_required"}` from outside the box without an ArozOS desktop session. A direct localhost request still works. Move any remote script to SSH and localhost.
- `POST /api/nylas/agent/import` returns `409 grant_already_set` when the box already has a different companion grant.
- The Google Calendar events and free-slots routes return 400 for a bad `date` or time zone.
- The last30days REST API is gone.
- New: `POST /joshu/api/research/reports` (with optional `web_provider`), `GET /joshu/api/connectors/mail/gmail/live-search?q=`, `POST /joshu/api/router/media`, and `POST /joshu/api/realtime-goals/:goalId/done-elsewhere` (direct localhost only).

### Rollback

To go back to 0.1.59, set the pins in `instance.env` back to 0.1.59, run the host bundle script of the 0.1.59 image, and recreate the services. The volumes keep the reports in `Research/`, but 0.1.59 cannot run them or the recurring reports. The last30days cron jobs and skills that 0.1.65 removed do not come back by themselves. The skills seed stamp of 0.1.65 is unknown to 0.1.59, so check the box skills after a rollback.

Fresh installs: [hetzner-quickstart.md](../vps-sandbox/hetzner-quickstart.md) (use tag `0.1.65`).

## Known issues

- On a self-hosted box, the action guard is off by default (`JOSHU_ACTION_GUARD_ENABLED`). Without Twilio texting and an owner mobile, the companion sends outside mail without asking the owner.
- The report PDFs, the report updates, and the Chat with files page show "joshu.me" in their footer.
- User manual chapter 10 still opens with two "Coming soon" notes, although research reports and themes are in this release. Its self-host block names the router API key but not the Exa key.
- A content goal kind that fails stays blocked for 7 days. To retry it sooner, remove its key from `references/routes.json` of skill `joshu-content-cache` in `$HERMES_HOME/skills/`.
- Joshu does not yet delete the files that the realtime goal broker copies under `<state dir>/files/`.
- Box changes to a moved section of `ea-playbook` that are not box pitfalls stay in the box copy of `ea-playbook`, and the LLM merge may drop them.
- A new realtime goal that repeats an open email request is not matched. Only mail ingress closes a realtime goal.
- The Hermes background skill review can still make a skill after a long run of the release facts check job.
- The Chat tab of the Hermes dashboard has no prebuilt TUI in the image, so Hermes builds it the first time someone opens the tab.
- `pnpm-lock.yaml` still lists `follow-redirects` 1.16.0. No build uses that file.

## User summary

Joshu OSS 0.1.65 in plain words, for the owner of a box:

- You can ask your companion for research on any topic, by text, email, chat, Slack, Telegram, or a phone call. You get a short summary and a PDF report with its sources, sorted into themes.
- You can ask for a regular update on topics you follow. The new Research app lists your reports and updates, and replaces the last30days app.
- You can ask for a report that uses You.com for its web search, to compare it with the usual search.
- With a content router key, your companion can read social posts that you send, make pictures and short videos, and add social posts to your reports.
- Your mail, connections, and setup answers can be read or changed only from your own Joshu desktop.
- Your companion can find every email in all your Gmail accounts, make Gmail drafts for you to send, and puts you on the guest list of meetings it books.
- In a Slack group chat you can talk to your companion by name, and files you send on Slack or Telegram are used for the task.
- Your companion reads the user manual when you ask how Joshu works.
