# Joshu OSS 0.1.65: what's in this release

## Research reports on any topic

You can ask your companion for research on any topic, by text message, email, chat, Slack, Telegram, or a phone call. A few minutes later you get a short summary and a full report as a PDF in the same conversation. The report sorts what your companion found into themes, each with a headline, a few paragraphs, and the articles it came from. If you name the sections you want, the report uses your sections. The reports are saved in the Research folder of your files.

## Regular updates on topics you follow

You can ask for a regular update in one message, for example "every Friday, send me what's new on my company and its competitors". Your companion then sends you what is new since the last update, by email or, when you ask, by text message, Slack, or Telegram. When nothing is new, it sends nothing. The new Research app on your desktop lists your reports and updates, and you can change, pause, or delete them there. The Research app replaces the last30days app, and topics you watched there move to one regular update.

## A second choice of web search

When you ask for a research report, you can ask your companion to search the web with You.com instead of the usual search, so you can compare the two on the same topic. The report tells you which search it used. If you don't ask, nothing changes. This choice needs a paid key that the person who runs your box sets up.

## Social posts, pictures, and videos

With that same key, your companion can read a social post that you send as a link, including what is said in a video and the comments. It can also make pictures and short videos, and add popular social posts to your reports. Each use takes a small amount from the balance of that key.

## Your data is better protected

Your mail, your connections, and your setup answers can now be read or changed only from your own Joshu desktop or by your companion itself.

## Mail, calendar, and chat

- Your companion can search all your Gmail accounts, also folders such as Updates and Spam, so it finds messages like sign-up confirmations.
- It can make drafts in your Gmail, and a draft waits until you send it yourself.
- When it books a meeting with other people, you are on the guest list.
- In a Slack group chat, you can talk to your companion by name, and files you send on Slack or Telegram are used for the task.
- If you send the same request by email that you sent on Slack or by text, your companion closes the old question and tells you it is done.
- When you ask how Joshu works, your companion reads the user manual and answers with its steps.
