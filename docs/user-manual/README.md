# The Joshu user's manual

Welcome to Joshu. The manual explains how to work with your companion, the AI assistant that works only for you. Most of the time, you just tell your companion what you want, in your own words, on the channel that is easiest for you.

The manual is for a box that you run yourself. You install the box on your own server, and you connect it to your own accounts: your domain, your AI keys, and, if you want them, your own Composio, Nylas, and Twilio accounts. To install a box, read the [self-host guide](../self-host.md) first. The manual then explains how to use the box, and each chapter says which of your accounts a feature needs.

## Five things to try first

1. In jChat, ask: "What's on my calendar tomorrow?"
2. Forward an email to your companion and ask: "What does this person want from me?"
3. Ask: "Remind me at 4 pm to call Sam."
4. In jChat, say "morning review" to plan your day together.
5. Copy your companion on an email to schedule a meeting.

The calendar and mail examples need your accounts in the **Connectors** app, and the email examples need your companion's own mailbox (see [Optional services](01-getting-started.md#optional-services)).
